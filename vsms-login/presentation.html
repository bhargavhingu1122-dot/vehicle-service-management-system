01 — Problem Statement

What Problem Are We Solving?

⚠️ The Problem

Traditional vehicle service management relies on phone calls, paper records, and manual coordination. Customer experience is fragmented, mechanics lack task visibility, and admins struggle to oversee operations.

  • No centralized system for booking & tracking services
  • Customers cannot view real-time service status
  • Manual assignment of mechanics is error-prone
  • No invoice or service history records
  • Poor communication between customers & shop

✅ Our Solution

  • Unified portal for customers, mechanics & admin
  • Real-time service tracking with timeline logs
  • Smart mechanic assignment by admin dashboard
  • Auto-generated invoices on job completion
  • Secure auth with OTP email verification

🏢 Real-World Use Case

Any automotive service centre, fleet management company, or car dealership that needs to digitise their service operations and provide customers a premium booking experience.

Method Endpoint Description
POST/api/registerRegister Customer or Mechanic with OTP verification
POST/api/loginCross-collection login (Admin / Customer / Mechanic)
POST/api/forgot-passwordSend 4-digit OTP to user email for password reset
POST/api/reset-passwordVerify OTP and update hashed password
GET/api/vehicles/:idFetch all vehicles owned by a customer
POST/api/service-requestsBook a new vehicle service request
PUT/api/admin/service-requests/:id/assignAdmin assigns mechanic to a job
DELETE/api/admin/user/:idAdmin deletes Customer or Mechanic
04 — Features & Functionality

Key Features

🔐
Authentication Module
Secure login & registration with role-based access (Admin, Customer, Mechanic). OTP email verification, Forgot Password + Reset flow, Change Password, and Google OAuth integration.
JWT-less Session bcrypt OTP Email Google OAuth
📋
Dashboard with CRUD
Role-specific dashboards. Admin can create/read/update/delete users, mechanic assignments, and service requests. Customers manage vehicles & services. Mechanics update job status.
Create Read Update Delete
🔍
Search & Pagination
Admin dashboard includes live search across users, mechanics, and service requests. Paginated tables ensure performance with large datasets and easy navigation through records.
Live Search Pagination Filtering
🚗
Vehicle Management
Customers can register multiple vehicles with details (brand, model, plate, year, fuel type). Full CRUD — add, edit, and delete vehicles. Unique license plate enforcement.
Multiple Vehicles Edit / Delete
🛠️
Service Booking
7 service categories (Periodic, Oil Change, AC Repair, Brake, Battery, Car Wash, Wheel Alignment). Choose date, time, and vehicle. Timeline logs track every status update.
7 Service Types Timeline Scheduling
🧾
Invoice & History
Invoices are auto-generated when a job is marked Completed. Customers view full service history with status, mechanic name, and cost. Pending / Paid invoice tracking.
Auto Invoice Service History
